About Lesotho

Lesotho is called the Kingdom in the Sky because much of the country is high above sea level. Sesotho is widely spoken. The Basotho blanket and mokorotlo hat are cultural symbols.

Things visitors can do

  • Visit natural places
  • Learn about Basotho culture
  • Take photographs
  • Try local food
